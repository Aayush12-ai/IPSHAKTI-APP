Build a complete, polished Android mobile application called “IP SAKTI” — a multilingual AI decision-support assistant for Ayurvedic Intellectual Property, regulatory guidance, Traditional Knowledge, biodiversity/ABS and commercialization.

The app should feel like a premium, trustworthy Indian government-tech product combined with a modern AI assistant.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Use Jetpack Compose + Material 3.

Visual style:

Clean
Minimal
Premium
Calm
Professional
Ayurveda-inspired without looking overly traditional
Modern AI product aesthetic

Use this color system:

Primary: Deep Forest Green #176B4D
Secondary: Ayurvedic Sage #6F9278
Accent: Warm Saffron #D89B3D
Background: Warm Off-White #F8F8F4
Surface: White #FFFFFF
Text: Deep Charcoal #1F2924

Use green as the primary brand color, saffron only for important highlights, and plenty of white space.

Avoid:

excessive gradients
neon colors
glassmorphism
excessive shadows
clutter
cartoonish Ayurveda illustrations
too many colors

Use rounded cards, subtle borders, restrained elevation and strong typography.

For uncertain/high-risk questions:

“Insufficient evidence for a reliable conclusion.”

Then provide:

Connect with an Expert

Never fabricate a law, citation, patent or regulatory requirement.

UX REQUIREMENTS

The app must feel:

Simple enough for an Ayurveda practitioner

but

powerful enough for an IP researcher.

Avoid overwhelming the user with legal terminology.

Use progressive disclosure:

Simple answer first → detailed evidence on demand.

Make primary actions obvious.

Keep navigation consistent.

Ensure readable contrast and accessible touch targets.

Use Material 3 components and responsive layouts rather than custom UI patterns where standard components are appropriate.

Build the complete navigable Android application with polished empty states, loading states, error states and realistic demo data.
